## Context

Features 42 and 43 built the v2 vocabulary (tokens, primitives, shader
backgrounds) and the chrome (header, mobile menu, footer, section anchors).
The home page still renders the v1 Hero, Propósito, Servicios and Contacto
sections on a flat Negro 950 canvas. This feature replaces the first three
with the four v2 sections the design draws above the contact block, and
retires the v1 ones. The contact block itself is feature 45.

### Settled decisions (cited, not re-decided)

- The home no longer has Propósito or Proyectos (`muush-1.0.md` § 1).
  Proyectos was never built (feature 15 stayed blocked), so only Propósito
  has to be removed from the page.
- Home order: Nav → Hero (listón) → Qué es muush (map) → Servicios → Cómo
  trabajamos (acanalado) → Diagnóstico y contacto → Footer (`muush-1.0.md`
  § 3). Diagnóstico y contacto is feature 45; until then the v1 contact
  section stays at `#diagnostico`.
- Map: tabs América / Europa on mobile with an animated zoom of the same map
  (not a PNG); full map on desktop with no tabs (pen extract § 2.1).
- Servicios: carousel on mobile, 4-column grid from 1024 px (`lg`).
- Cómo trabajamos: the line fills with scroll; this is the handoff's
  "starting point", Clau may refine it later (`muush-1.0.md` § 5).
- Step 01's description is the short sentence on mobile and the full one on
  desktop (Roberto, 2026-11-01, pen extract § 7).
- The primary CTA reads "Diagnóstico gratis" / "Free assessment".
- Everything respects `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- 390 px and 1440 px must match the `.pen`; other widths may adapt sizes and
  spacing, never colour, components, copy or behaviour (AGENTS.md § 3.1).
- No version bump in this feature.

- Q: How does the drawn gradient line relate to the red scroll fill? → A: The
  gradient is the permanent track; a solid Red muush 500 fill grows over it

## User Scenarios & Testing *(mandatory)*

"Visitor" is anyone on `/es` or `/en`. "Reviewer" is Roberto, approving the
home in the component catalogue and on the `muush-v2` build.

### User Story 1 - Understand what muush offers at first glance (Priority: P1)

A visitor lands on the home and, over the moving listón, reads the rotating
pill of services, the two-tone headline "Hablamos negocio / y código.", the
subtitle and two actions: "Diagnóstico gratis" and "Cómo trabajamos".

**Why this priority**: the hero is the first screen every visitor sees; it is
the page's main heading and its main conversion path.

**Independent Test**: render the hero at 390 and 1440 in ES and EN in the
catalogue and on the integration build; compare with pen extract § 1; follow
both actions.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** a 1440 px window on `/es`, **When** the page loads, **Then** the
   hero is 1000 tall over the listón, its content column starts at y 290 and
   shows, centred with gap 28: the rotating pill, the 96 px headline (line 1
   Blanco, line 2 Gris 400), the 20 px subtitle at 640 wide and the two
   buttons with gap 14.
2. **Given** `/en`, **When** the page loads, **Then** it reads "We speak
   business / and code.", "We design and build the digital solutions your
   business uses every day.", "Free assessment" and "How we work", and the
   pill rotates through the four English service names.
3. **Given** a 390 px window, **When** the page loads, **Then** the hero is
   740 tall with padding 110 / 24 / 60, everything centred with gap 22, the
   headline at 34 px, the subtitle at 17 px full width and both buttons in
   mobile size (padding 12 / 14, text 14, the primary keeping its 20 px
   arrow), side by side in one centred row with gap 8 and 8 above; where the
   row does not fit the content width (e.g. 360 px) the second button wraps
   below, centred, with the same gap (Clarifications Q1, confirmed by Roberto 2026-11-01).
4. **Given** any width, **When** the visitor activates "Diagnóstico gratis",
   **Then** they reach the `#diagnostico` section of the same page; **When**
   they activate "Cómo trabajamos", **Then** they reach `#como-trabajamos`.
5. **Given** reduced motion, **When** the page loads, **Then** the listón is a
   still frame and the pill stays on its first service.
6. **Given** scripting is off or WebGL is unavailable, **When** the page
   loads, **Then** the hero's poster shows, every string is in the
   prerendered HTML and both actions work as plain links.

### User Story 2 - See where the team is (Priority: P1)

A visitor reads "Qué es muush": a pill, a two-line heading, a dotted world map
with red pins for the team and steel pins for partners, a paragraph and a
legend. On a phone the map is too wide, so two tabs (América / Europa) zoom
the same map into each region.

**Why this priority**: it is the section that explains the team model, and
the map is the feature's most complex new component; it is also reused by
Nosotros in feature 46.

**Independent Test**: render the section at 390, 768, 1024 and 1440 in ES and
EN; operate the tabs with touch and keyboard; turn reduced motion on; check the
network panel for any map request.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** 1440 px, **When** the section renders, **Then** it shows, centred
   with gap 28 and padding 110 / 0: the pill "Qué es muush", the 56 px heading,
   the 1408 × 470 map at 80 % opacity with all nine pins, the 17 px body text
   in Gris 400 at 640 wide, and the legend (Equipo with a Red muush 500 dot,
   Aliados with an Acero 200 dot, "Trabajamos con clientes en cualquier
   país.") with gap 22. No tabs are present.
2. **Given** any width from `lg` up, **When** the section renders, **Then** the
   whole map is visible, scaled to the available width with its proportions
   kept, and no tab is shown.
3. **Given** a width below `lg`, **When** the section renders, **Then** the
   order is pill, heading (28 px, centred), the selector, the 342 × 240 map box
   (radius 16) at 390, the body text in Blanco 70 % and the legend with the
   extra "Estonia · pronto" chip; "América" is selected and the box shows the
   America region of the same map.
4. **Given** the selector, **When** the visitor activates "Europa", **Then**
   the map moves and zooms smoothly to the Europe region, "Europa" becomes the
   active tab (Blanco 10 % fill, 14 / 600 Blanco) and "América" inactive
   (transparent, Gris 400).
5. **Given** a keyboard user on the selector, **When** they press the arrow
   keys, Home or End, **Then** focus and selection move between the tabs per
   the tablist pattern, the selected tab is the only one in the tab order, and
   the change is announced as a tab selection.
6. **Given** reduced motion, **When** the region changes, **Then** the map
   jumps to the new region with no animation.
7. **Given** any width, **When** the page loads, **Then** the map makes no
   network request of its own: the dots and pins are part of the page.
8. **Given** a screen-reader user, **When** they reach the map, **Then** the
   dots are silent and the pins are announced as a visually hidden list of
   cities grouped under the legend labels ("Equipo", "Aliados", "Estonia ·
   pronto"); below `lg` the list in the tab panel holds only the selected
   region's cities, and the tab list is named "Región del mapa" / "Map
   region" (Clarifications Q2, confirmed by Roberto 2026-11-01).

### User Story 3 - Browse the services (Priority: P1)

A visitor reads "Lo que hacemos / por tu negocio." and four service cards,
each with a glass icon tile over a coloured edge light, a title and a short
description, then the "También, si lo necesitas" strip about Creative and
Marketing. On a phone the cards are a swipeable carousel with an indicator.

**Why this priority**: Servicios is the target of the main nav link and the
core of what the site sells.

**Independent Test**: render the section at 390, 768, 1024 and 1440 in ES and
EN; swipe, scroll with a trackpad and tab through the carousel; watch the
indicator.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** 1440 px, **When** the section renders, **Then** it has padding
   120 / 80 and gap 44: a left-aligned head (pill "Servicios", 56 px heading,
   gap 22), four cards in a row with gap 20, top-aligned, each card's height
   following its content, and the 1280-wide strip.
2. **Given** a card, **When** it renders, **Then** it is the `servicio` glass
   surface (Negro 900, radius 24, top-lit 1 px edge), with a 210-tall visual
   area holding its edge light and the centred 96 × 96 glass tile with its
   40 px icon in Blanco, and a text block (24 px Poppins title, 15 px
   description in Blanco 70 %). The four cards use, in order: `compass`
   (Crimson 600 light from the bottom-left), `layout-dashboard` (Acero 400
   from the bottom-right), `workflow` (Crimson 600 from the bottom-left),
   `pen-tool` (Arena 300 from the top-right).
3. **Given** 1440 px, **When** the cards render, **Then** the titles break as
   drawn: "Software, web / y nube" and "Diseño de producto / y UX" (and their
   English counterparts at the matching point).
4. **Given** the strip at `lg` and up, **When** it renders, **Then** it shows
   the "TAMBIÉN, SI LO NECESITAS" label, the chips "Creativo" and
   "Marketing" (each with a 6 px Acero 200 dot) on the left and the note on the
   right.
5. **Given** a width below `lg`, **When** the section renders, **Then** the
   cards form a horizontal carousel starting at the gutter, 290 wide with gap
   14, the next card peeking in, snapping card by card, followed by a
   4-dot indicator and the stacked "También" card (label, "Creativo ·
   Marketing" in Poppins 18, note in Blanco 70 %).
6. **Given** the carousel, **When** the visitor scrolls it by any means
   (finger, trackpad, keyboard), **Then** the indicator marks the card that is
   in view: the active mark is a 22 × 6 Red muush 500 pill, the others 6 × 6
   dots in Blanco 25 %; at the end of the scroll the last mark is active.
7. **Given** a width of 1024 px or more, **When** the section renders,
   **Then** the cards are a 4-column grid and no indicator is shown.
8. **Given** the nav link "Servicios", **When** it is activated, **Then** it
   lands on this section (it carries `#servicios`).

### User Story 4 - Follow how we work (Priority: P2)

A visitor scrolls into "Cómo trabajamos" over the vidrio acanalado and sees
five steps on a line. As they scroll, the line fills in red and each step
lights up when the fill reaches it. On a phone the steps are a vertical rail.

**Why this priority**: it is the target of the hero's secondary action and of
the footer link; its animation is explicitly a starting point.

**Independent Test**: scroll the section at 390 and 1440 in ES and EN, with
and without reduced motion; check every step reaches its lit state.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** 1440 px, **When** the section renders, **Then** it is 730 tall
   over the acanalado, with padding 120 / 80 and gap 64: a centred head (pill
   "Cómo trabajamos", 52 px heading, gap 22) and the 1280-wide process with
   five equal columns (gap 20), each column showing dot, number, title,
   description and commitment chip (gap 16), with the 2 px line behind the
   dots.
2. **Given** a step that is not lit, **When** it renders, **Then** its dot is a
   Blanco 6 % halo with a 12 px Negro 950 core and a 1.5 px Blanco 40 % stroke,
   its number Gris 400 and its description Gris 400; **given** a lit step, its
   dot is a Red 20 % halo with a 12 px Red muush 500 core, its number Coral 300
   and its description Blanco 85 %. Titles are Blanco in both states.
3. **Given** the visitor scrolls down through the section, **When** the fill
   advances, **Then** the line fills from the left (top on mobile) and each
   step switches to lit as the fill reaches it; scrolling back up reverses it.
4. **Given** reduced motion, **When** the section renders, **Then** the line
   is fully filled and all five steps are lit from the start, with no
   animation.
5. **Given** scripting is off, **When** the page loads, **Then** all five
   steps are readable, and they show lit (the fully-lit state is the no-script
   state).
6. **Given** each commitment chip, **When** it renders, **Then** it shows its
   icon (`message-circle-warning`, `sliders-horizontal`, `user-round`,
   `file-text`, `key-round`) in a 22 px red-tinted bubble and its 13 px text,
   identical in both states.
7. **Given** a width below `lg`, **When** the section renders, **Then** it is
   a vertical rail (padding 72 / 24, gap 24, heading 28 px left-aligned): each
   step a row with a 14 px rail (12 px dot and, except the last, a 1 × 100
   line in Blanco 20 %), then the title "01  Diagnóstico" in Poppins 17, the
   14 px description in Blanco 70 % and the mobile chip (Red 10 % fill, Red
   25 % stroke, 5 px red dot, 12 px text, no icon).
8. **Given** a width below `lg`, **When** step 01 renders, **Then** its
   description is the short sentence ("Una revisión rápida para ver qué
   conviene resolver." / "A quick review to see what's worth solving."); from
   `lg` up it is the full sentence.
9. **Given** the desktop line, **When** it renders, **Then** the drawn
   gradient (Red muush 500 → Coral 300 → Arena 300 → Acero 200 →
   transparent) is always visible as the track, and the scroll fill is a
   solid Red muush 500 line drawn over it from the left up to the progress
   point; on mobile the rail segments (Blanco 20 %) are the track
   (Clarifications Q3, confirmed by Roberto 2026-11-01).

### User Story 5 - The page no longer shows retired sections (Priority: P2)

A visitor sees no trace of the v1 Hero, Propósito or v1 Servicios; the v1
contact section still closes the page until feature 45. Every link on the page
and in the shell lands on a section that exists. The page keeps its search
metadata.

**Why this priority**: retiring v1 is the feature's other half; a dead anchor
or a broken SEO tag would ship to production at the 47 merge.

**Independent Test**: inspect the generated `/es` and `/en` HTML: section ids,
link targets, title, description, canonical, hreflang, structured data and
sitemap entries; open the catalogue's `Legacy v1/` group.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** `/es` or `/en`, **When** the page renders, **Then** its sections
   are, in order: Hero, Qué es muush, Servicios (`#servicios`), Cómo trabajamos
   (`#como-trabajamos`), the v1 contact section (`#diagnostico`), then the
   footer. No Propósito and no Proyectos.
2. **Given** every link in the generated home, header, menu and footer,
   **When** its fragment is checked, **Then** it names an id present on the
   target page; no link points at `#proposito`, `#proyectos` or any other
   removed id from the home.
3. **Given** the generated home in each locale, **When** its metadata is
   compared with feature 35's, **Then** the title, description, canonical,
   hreflang alternates, robots directives, structured data and sitemap entry
   are unchanged, and the page has exactly one `h1` (the hero headline).
4. **Given** the component catalogue, **When** the reviewer opens it, **Then**
   the retired v1 sections (and the still-rendered v1 contact section) are
   listed only under `Legacy v1/`, and the v2 home sections are listed outside
   it, each with ES and EN stories at 390 and 1440.

### User Story 6 - The logo is not announced as the current page (Priority: P3)

On the home page, a screen-reader user hears "current page" once, on the
"Inicio" nav item, not also on the logo.

**Why this priority**: an accessibility nit inherited from feature 43's review
(review-43c nit 5); small, but it ships in the shell on every page.

**Independent Test**: inspect `aria-current` on `/es` and `/en` (generated HTML
and after hydration).

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** `/es` or `/en` at the top of the page, **When** the header
   renders, **Then** the logo link carries no `aria-current` and the "Inicio" /
   "Home" nav item carries `aria-current="page"`.
2. **Given** any page, **When** any logo link renders (header, open mobile
   menu, desktop and mobile footer), **Then** it carries no `aria-current`; the
   nav items keep their feature-43 behaviour.

### Edge Cases

- **Widths between the anchors** (360, 768, 1024, 1280, 1920): no horizontal
  scroll, no clipped text, no overlap. The hero headline and section headings
  scale fluidly; the desktop map scales down below 1440 and is capped at its
  drawn 1408 above it; the cards keep four columns from 1024; at 1920 the
  content stays within the 1280 content width.
- **Tablet (768–1023)**: still the mobile arrangement (tabs, carousel, rail),
  since all three switch at `lg`; the map box and the carousel use the wider
  space without breaking proportions.
- **Carousel at the end**: when the last card cannot scroll to the start
  position, the indicator still marks the last card.
- **Carousel and focus**: tabbing into a card that is off-screen scrolls it
  into view and the indicator follows.
- **Fast scroll / jump to `#como-trabajamos`**: arriving by a link sets the
  correct fill and lit steps immediately, not only after further scrolling.
- **Resize across `lg`** with a region selected: the desktop map shows whole;
  going back below `lg` shows the selected region again.
- **Language switch** inside any section: the feature-43 position restore
  works with the new sections (the anchored sections keep the same ids in both
  locales).
- **No WebGL / shader failure**: the hero and Cómo trabajamos show their
  posters; text stays legible.
- **Forced colours**: tabs, indicator and lit steps remain distinguishable
  (selection is not conveyed by colour alone: the active tab and indicator mark
  also change shape/fill).
- **Print**: the shader backgrounds are not printed (feature 42 behaviour).

## Requirements *(mandatory)*

### Functional Requirements

#### Page composition

- **FR-001**: The home in both locales MUST render, in order: Hero, Qué es
  muush, Servicios, Cómo trabajamos, the v1 contact section, under the
  feature-43 shell. Sections sit flush; each carries its own padding as drawn.
- **FR-002**: Servicios MUST carry the `servicios` anchor and Cómo trabajamos
  the `como-trabajamos` anchor, both taken from the shared section-anchor list
  (feature 43). The v1 contact section keeps `diagnostico`.
- **FR-003**: Only the Hero and Cómo trabajamos MUST have animated
  backgrounds (`homeHero` and `howWeWork` presets). Qué es muush and Servicios
  sit on the Negro 950 canvas.

#### Hero

- **FR-004**: The hero MUST be 1000 tall at 1440 and 740 at 390, over the
  listón, with the content column described in pen extract § 1, using the
  shared RotatingPill, Heading (`hero` size, level 1), PrimaryButton (hero
  size) and GlassButton.
- **FR-005**: The headline's second line MUST be Gris 400 at both widths (pen
  extract § 1 note) and the subtitle Blanco 70 %.
- **FR-006**: "Diagnóstico gratis" MUST link to `#diagnostico` and "Cómo
  trabajamos" to `#como-trabajamos` on the current locale's home, as links
  that work without scripting.

#### Qué es muush

- **FR-007**: The map MUST be the dotted world map supplied by the leader
  (1408 × 470, 1693 dots), shipped as part of the site, in Blanco at about
  18 %, with no external request.
- **FR-008**: Pins MUST come from a data list (city, position in map
  coordinates, kind), never be drawn by hand in markup. The nine pins and kinds
  are those of `map-pins.json`: team (CDMX, Puebla, Mérida, Dublín, NYC, San
  Francisco, Los Ángeles), partner (Ámsterdam), soon (Estonia).
- **FR-009** (amended 2026-11-01, see § Amendments): Each pin MUST be an 8 px
  dot centred in a 28 × 28 footprint (team Red muush 500, partner Acero 200,
  soon a hollow 8 px ring in Gris 400). The team and partner dots carry the
  radar ping; the drawn halos are not painted as static rings.
- **FR-010**: From `lg` up the full map MUST show at 80 % opacity with no
  tabs; below `lg` a two-tab selector (América / Europa) MUST zoom the same map
  into the region of the selected tab, using the handoff's regions (America
  210, 95, 285 × 200; Europe 645, 75, 190 × 95 in map coordinates).
- **FR-011a**: The pins MUST be exposed as a visually hidden list of city
  names grouped under the legend labels (all cities from `lg`; the selected
  region's cities in the tab panel below `lg`); the dots MUST be hidden from
  assistive technology; the tab list MUST be named from i18n ("Región del
  mapa" / "Map region"). Pins keep 28 × 28 on screen at every scale.
- **FR-011**: The selector MUST follow the WAI-ARIA tabs pattern (tab list,
  tabs with selected state, one tab panel holding the map, roving focus with
  arrow keys, Home and End) and be fully keyboard operable.
- **FR-012**: The region change MUST animate smoothly with the shared slow
  duration and easing, and MUST be instant under reduced motion.
- **FR-013**: The mobile legend MUST add the "Estonia · pronto" / "Estonia ·
  soon" chip with the hollow Estonia marker; the desktop legend does not have
  it.

#### Servicios

- **FR-014**: The four cards MUST use the shared `servicio` glass surface and
  the visual area, tile, icon and text block of pen extract § 3.1, with the
  per-card icon and edge light listed there.
- **FR-015**: Below `lg` the cards MUST be a horizontally scrolling carousel
  with mandatory snapping, the next card peeking in; from `lg` they MUST be a
  4-column grid.
- **FR-016**: The carousel indicator MUST reflect the card in view at all
  times while scrolling, by any input, including the last card at the end of
  the scroll; it is hidden from `lg`. It is a visual aid only, hidden from
  assistive technology, since every card is reachable in reading order.
- **FR-017**: The "También, si lo necesitas" strip MUST render as drawn at
  `lg` and up (strip with label, two chips and note) and as the stacked card
  below `lg`.

#### Cómo trabajamos

- **FR-018**: The section MUST render the five steps of § 4.5 copy with the
  lit and not-lit states of pen extract § 4, horizontal from `lg` and as a
  vertical rail below.
- **FR-018a**: On desktop the drawn gradient line MUST stay visible as the
  track and the fill MUST be solid Red muush 500 over it; on mobile the
  Blanco 20 % rail segments are the track. *(Desktop part superseded
  2026-11-02 by FR-044: the desktop track is Blanco 20 %, no gradient.)*
- **FR-019**: A progress value MUST advance with the visitor's scroll through
  the section and fill the line (left to right on desktop, top to bottom on
  mobile); each step MUST switch to lit when the fill reaches it and back when
  it recedes. The trigger points follow the handoff's starting-point
  animation. *(Desktop part superseded 2026-11-02 by FR-042…FR-045: no
  desktop scroll fill; the mobile rail keeps this.)*
- **FR-020**: Under reduced motion, and before or without scripting, the line
  MUST be fully filled and every step lit. *(Mobile rail only since
  2026-11-02; the desktop rests unlit, FR-044.)*
- **FR-021**: The commitment chip MUST be identical in both states; desktop
  chips carry their lucide icon, mobile chips a 5 px red dot and no icon.
- **FR-022**: Step 01's description MUST be the short sentence below `lg` and
  the full sentence from `lg` up; both sentences come from the locale files.

#### Retirement of v1

- **FR-023**: The v1 Hero, Propósito and Servicios sections MUST no longer
  render on the home. Their components and stories MUST move to a quarantine
  location with stories under `Legacy v1/` (mirroring features 42 and 43),
  until feature 47 deletes them. The v1 contact section, still rendered,
