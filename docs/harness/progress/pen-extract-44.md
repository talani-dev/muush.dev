## 9 · Radar ping on the markers (Roberto, 2026-11-01): the leader misread the design

The halos the `.pen` draws around the red dots are NOT static rings. They
are how a static mockup draws the v1 **radar ping**, the same reading v1
made (`docs/business/landing/ui-map.md` § "Receta del ping del radar" and
`app/shared/ui/legacy/Radar.vue`).

Roberto wants the same effect in v2 on:
- the **dot of every Pill**, including the RotatingPill;
- the **map pins**.

Rebuild it in v2. Do not import from `legacy/`.

**Resting state:** only the solid dot, centred in its footprint. Do NOT
render the drawn halos as rings.

| Marker | Footprint | Dot |
|---|---|---|
| Pill | 20 | 7 |
| Map pin | 28 | 8 |

**Ping**
- A ghost element the size of the dot, in the dot's solid colour. The
  animation does the fading, not the colour.
- Scale `1 → 3`, opacity `0.6 → 0`.
- 2.4 s, `ease-out`, infinite.
- The ghost may overflow the footprint, which gives the radar-sweep look.
- One duration token, as in v1.

**Colour**
- Red muush 500 for the Pill and the team pins.
- Acero 200 for the partner pin (Ámsterdam).
- The "soon" pin (Estonia, hollow ring) gets **no** ping.

**Reduced motion:** no ping, and the dot stays fully visible.

**Footprints stay the same.** Pill height 36 and pin 28×28 must not change.
