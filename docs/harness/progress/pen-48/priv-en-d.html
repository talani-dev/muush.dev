Who is responsible for your data
Roberto Mirón Nájera, an individual operating under the trade name muush ("muush" or "we"), located at Del Puente #11, San Bartolo Ameyalco, Álvaro Obregón, 01800, Mexico City, Mexico, is responsible for processing your personal data. For any privacy matter, write to support@muush.dev.
What data we collect
Assessment form
Name, email, company or project, how you identify, your website or social media, what you need, preferred contact method and, if you choose WhatsApp, your number.
Work with muush form
Name, email, area and role, portfolio link, LinkedIn link, CV in PDF, preferred contact method and, if you choose WhatsApp, your number.
When you book a call
Name, email and the time you choose, through Cal.com.
While browsing the site
IP address, browser, pages visited, cookie identifiers and actions on the site.
We don't ask for sensitive personal data. A CV may include it without us asking, such as health status, religious beliefs or union membership. Please leave it out. If your CV includes it, we use it only to evaluate your profile and with your express consent, which you give by ticking the box on the form.
What we use it for
We use your data for these purposes, which we need to handle what you asked for:
If you requested an assessment: to answer your request, contact you the way you chose, prepare your assessment and a proposal and, if we work together, manage the business relationship and invoicing.
If you applied through Work with muush: to evaluate your profile with your express consent, contact you for an interview and invite you to projects that fit for as long as we keep your CV.
In both cases: to send you the automatic email confirming we received your information.
We also use browsing data for one additional purpose, which we don't need to handle your request:
Measuring and showing advertising: we use pixels, such as Meta Pixel, to see how our ads perform and to show muush ads to people who visited the site.
You can object to this purpose at any time, as explained in Cookies and pixels. We don't use what you write in the forms or your CV for advertising.
Who we share it with
We don't sell your data. We only share it with:
The providers that help us operate: Amazon Web Services (site hosting), [email delivery service], Google Drive (CV storage), Notion (records of requests and applications) and Cal.com (call scheduling). They process your data on our behalf, under our instructions and under confidentiality. Some have servers outside Mexico.
Meta Platforms, which receives browsing data through its pixel to measure and show ads.
muush collaborators working on your project, only the data they need and under a confidentiality agreement. Some may be outside Mexico.
Authorities, when a law requires it.
Your talent pool profile and CV stay at muush: we don't share them with partners or other companies.
Except for what is stated about Meta, we don't make any data transfers that require your consent.
How long we keep it
Your CV and talent pool profile: 1 year from when we receive them. When that period ends, we delete them.
Assessment requests that don't move forward: 12 months from the last contact.
Clients: for the length of the relationship and, after that, the period required by tax law.
Browsing and pixel data: [period set by each tool].
You can ask us to delete it sooner at any time.
Your rights
You have the right to access, rectify or update, cancel or object to the use of your data (known in Mexico as ARCO rights). You can also object to decisions made solely by automated means; we don't make any today.
To exercise them, email support@muush.dev with the subject "ARCO rights" and include:
1
Your name and the email where you want our reply.
2
A copy of your official ID. If someone represents you, their ID and the document that proves it.
3
Which data and which right you want to exercise. For a correction, the correct data.
4
Any document that helps us find your data.
We reply within 20 business days at most. If your request is valid, we carry it out within the following 15 business days. It's free.
How to withdraw consent or limit the use of your data
Email support@muush.dev with the subject "Withdraw consent" or "Limit use of my data", with the same details as for ARCO rights. Without your data, we may not be able to keep handling your request or your application.
Cookies and pixels
muush.dev uses the technical cookies it needs to work, for example to remember your language. If you accept them, it also uses Meta Pixel to measure visits and ads. These technologies collect your IP address, browser, pages visited and cookie identifiers.
You can accept or decline them in the site's cookie notice and change your choice here at any time. You can also turn them off in your browser settings or in your Meta account's ad preferences.
