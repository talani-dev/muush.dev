## 1 · What was built

### Pages and routes
- `app/pages/privacy.vue` and `app/pages/terms.vue` are thin wrappers. `nuxt.config.ts` `i18n.pages` adds `privacy` (`/es/privacidad` ↔ `/en/privacy`) and `terms` (`/es/terminos` ↔ `/en/terms`).
- Both pages are indexed: no noindex, canonical and hreflang come from the layout, `usePageSeo` pins a plain `WebPage`. The sitemap now lists 10 URLs; the crawler finds the pages through the footer.
- The locale toggle resolves the pages through the route map. Checked in the browser: on `/es/privacidad` the toggle points to `/en/privacy`, and the other way round.

### New feature module `app/features/legal/` (ui / logic / data / `index.ts`)
- **data:**
  - `privacyNotice.ts` and `termsAndConditions.ts` describe each page as i18n keys: sections, anchors and the block kinds `paragraph`, `bullets`, `steps`, `table` and `preferences`.
  - `cookieCategories.ts` holds the categories as data (`OPTIONAL_COOKIE_CATEGORIES = ['advertising']`), plus the record type, the storage key and the version.
  - `types.ts`.
- **logic:**
  - `consentRecord.ts`: pure functions that build and defensively parse the stored record.
  - `useCookieConsent.ts`: module-scoped, client-only state. This is **the seam feature 49 reads**: `isGranted('advertising')`, `consent`, `hasDecided`, plus `openPreferences`, `closePreferences`, `saveChoice`, `acceptAll` and `rejectAll`.
  - `useCookieConsentContent.ts`.
  - `resolveLegalPage.ts` (pure) and `useLegalPageContent.ts`.
  - `activeSection.ts` (pure) and `useLegalScrollSpy.ts`.
- **ui:**
  - Legal pages: `LegalArticle`, `LegalSection`, `LegalList`, `LegalDataTable` (a table from `md`, cards below it), `LegalIndex` (sticky desktop index with the scroll-spy dot) and `LegalIndexDisclosure` (a native `<details>` "En esta página" below `lg`).
  - Consent: `CookieBanner`, `CookiePreferences` (a modal `<dialog>`), `CookieCategoryRow`, `ConsentSwitch` (`role="switch"`), `ChoiceButton`, and `CookieConsent` (the wired host).
- **Storage:** `localStorage["muush.cookie-consent"]` holds `{version: 1, decidedAt, categories: {advertising}}`. A record that misses a category counts as "no choice", so adding "Analítica" shows the banner again by itself. Adding it means one entry in `cookieCategories.ts` plus two locale keys.

### S1 · ARCO list semantics: `LegalList.vue`
- The `<ol>` / `<ul>` now carries `role="list"`, so Safari/VoiceOver keeps the list semantics that preflight's `list-style: none` strips. The aria-hidden step numbers made this matter.
- **Lint trap:** a literal `role="list"` fails `pnpm check`. On `<component :is>`, Biome reads an unknown element and fires `noInteractiveElementToNoninteractiveRole` and `useSemanticElements`. On an explicit `<ul>`, it fires `noRedundantRoles`. A `<!-- biome-ignore -->` above the root becomes a second root node in dev builds: the component turned into a fragment and 3 tests broke. The role is therefore bound through `v-bind="LIST_SEMANTICS"`, and a comment next to it explains why.
- **Test:** `LegalList.test.ts`, "should keep list semantics under preflight…", covers both kinds.
- **Browser (Chrome AX tree, 390 and 1440, ES and EN):** the article `ol` and `ul` expose role `list`, and the built HTML has `role="list"` on all of them.
